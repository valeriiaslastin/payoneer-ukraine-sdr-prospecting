# Payoneer — Ukraine Market SDR Prospecting Model

An independent, self-directed sales-development (SDR) prospecting workbook built around Payoneer's real, publicly documented service for Ukrainian businesses.

- **Author:** Valeriia Slastin
- **Type:** Independent portfolio project — not affiliated with, commissioned by, or reviewed by Payoneer.
- **Primary deliverable:** Excel workbook (`Payoneer_Ukraine_SDR_Prospecting.xlsx`)

## About the Project

This project demonstrates the research, lead-scoring, and outreach workflow a Sales Development Representative targeting Ukrainian SMEs for Payoneer would realistically build. Payoneer was chosen specifically because it is verified to serve Ukrainian-registered companies directly — an earlier version of this project targeted a different fintech that turned out not to operate in Ukraine, and this version replaces it after that was caught and corrected.

The target market is Ukraine's IT services, software outsourcing, and e-commerce sector — companies that invoice international clients in USD/EUR and need fast multi-currency accounts.

## What's Inside

1. **README** — project scope and disclaimers
2. **ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)** — who the target account is and why
3. **Target Accounts (Ukraine)** — 15 real, named, verifiable companies, each with a live weighted fit score
4. **Lead Scoring Model** — the formula-driven weighting logic behind those scores
5. **Outreach Sequence (EN)** — a 4-touch cold email + LinkedIn cadence
6. **Outreach Sequence (UA)** — a genuinely localized (not machine-translated) Ukrainian version, with notes on register and channel choice
7. **Mock CRM Pipeline** — illustrative stages with clearly labeled, non-fabricated conversion assumptions
8. **Methodology & Caveats** — what's verified vs. estimated, and why

## Data Honesty Note

Company names, industries, and websites are real and independently verifiable. Employee counts and fit scores are researcher estimates, clearly labeled and confidence-rated. No claim is made that this represents a real Payoneer sales engagement or real outreach results.
